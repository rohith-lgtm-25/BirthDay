# Vindhya's Magical Birthday Surprise 🎂
## An Interactive Birthday Adventure Featuring Vir — Her Little German Shepherd Friend 🐶

Welcome to Vindhya's personalized, interactive birthday website!

---

### ✨ Highlights & Features

1. **Strict Dreamy Palette**:
   - Built exclusively using **lavender and shades of pink** (light lavender, blush pink, rose pink, soft mauve, baby pink, and pure white highlights).
   - Strictly zero blue, green, orange, or yellow anywhere — even the candle flames burn with magical pink-lavender fire with white-hot centers, and the foliage is soft pastel mauve.

2. **Main Character — Vir 🐶**:
   - An original, adorable cartoon German Shepherd puppy mascot.
   - Features signature black-and-tan coat markings, large upright pointed ears with blush pink interiors, big sparkling puppy eyes with blinking animation, button nose, friendly smile, and a wagging tail.
   - Illustrated with clean, scalable, layered vector SVGs and smooth CSS keyframe animations (walking, wagging, blinking, talking, jumping, sitting beside the cake, relaxing on a pink velvet cushion, and celebrating with a party hat!).
   - Natural typewriter dialogue engine with cute blip sound effects. Clicking the speech bubble instantly finishes typing for fast readers.

3. **Multi-Page Magical Journey**:
   - **Page 1: Vir's Magical Entrance & Cake Interaction**:
     - Vir walks into the pastel garden from the left, stops in the center, wags his tail, and speaks to Vindhya.
     - Interactive `[HELLO VIR! 💗]` button triggers Vir's happy jump.
     - 2-Tier pastel birthday cake appears with glowing candles.
     - Clicking `[BLOW THE CANDLES 🎂]` extinguishes the flames with realistic smoke trails, plays a gentle blowing sound, dims the screen into a suspenseful nighttime lavender glow, and then bursts into a celebration with glowing fairy lights, floating balloons, confetti cannon, and the grand reveal: **"HAPPY BIRTHDAY, VINDHYA!"**.
   - **Page 2: A Beautiful Friendship Story**:
     - Fairytale storybook with Vir sitting beside it.
     - Animated vertical timeline with 5 chapters: *The Beginning*, *Childhood Memories*, *Growing Up*, *The Friendship*, and *The Journey Ahead*.
     - Custom pink/lavender chapter emblems and concluding words from Vir.
   - **Page 3: What Makes Vindhya Special**:
     - Vir cozying up on a plush pink velvet cushion with pearl tufting and side tassels.
     - 6 glowing cards with custom badges: *Your Smile*, *Your Kindness*, *Your Strength*, *Your Personality*, *Your Dreams*, and *A Friend's Promise*.
   - **Page 4: Vir's Heartfelt Letter & Sky Lantern Wishing Well**:
     - Interactive wax-sealed envelope that opens on click to reveal a heartfelt birthday letter for Vindhya.
     - Interactive Sky Lantern Wishing Well: Vindhya can type her birthday wish and watch a glowing lantern rise into the starry twilight sky.
     - Interactive buttons to pet Vir's head (he barks and wags his tail happily!), shower more confetti, or replay the entire adventure.

4. **Procedural Web Audio Engine**:
   - Synthesizes soothing, music-box birthday melodies and realistic sound effects (puppy yip/bark, candle blow whoosh, confetti pop, celestial chimes, and typewriter blips) via the Web Audio API without needing external MP3 dependencies that could fail or load slowly.
   - Floating audio toggle pills in the header with animated sound wave bars.

---

### 🚀 How to Run

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser:

```bash
# Option 1: Open directly in your default browser (macOS)
open index.html

# Option 2: Run a local static server
python3 -m http.server 8080
# Then visit: http://localhost:8080 in your browser
```

Deep links supported:
- `http://localhost:8080/#cake` (Jump directly to Cake stage)
- `http://localhost:8080/#page-2` (Storybook timeline)
- `http://localhost:8080/#page-3` (Special Vindhya cards)
- `http://localhost:8080/#page-4` (Letter & Sky Lantern)
